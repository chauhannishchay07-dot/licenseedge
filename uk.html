The UK Problem

Old Contracts.
New Prices. No Review.

Many UK SMBs signed vendor contracts in 2019–2021 during a period of competitive SaaS pricing. Those contracts have since auto-renewed — often with 10–20% price increases baked in — and most businesses haven't had the time or context to challenge them.

Post-Brexit complexity added another layer: some vendors restructured their EU/UK pricing, others created separate UK entities with different terms. The result is a messy, expensive software stack that nobody has properly reviewed since pre-pandemic.

We know UK market rates, understand Making Tax Digital (MTD) requirements, and help you renegotiate from a position of knowledge — not guesswork.
