The Canadian Problem

Your Software Bill
Is in Two Languages.

Canadian businesses uniquely face software billing in two currencies. US-headquartered tools — which dominate the market — charge in USD. Local Canadian tools charge in CAD. Many businesses don't even know which of their subscriptions are which.

When the CAD weakens against the USD, every USD-billed tool gets silently more expensive. There's no notification, no re-evaluation — just a higher effective cost that erodes your budget month by month.

We map your full stack in both currencies, calculate the real CAD cost of every USD tool, and identify where Canadian alternatives or better pricing tiers would save you money.
