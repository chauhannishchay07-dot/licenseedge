The AU Problem

You're Paying US
Prices. In AUD.

Most business software is priced in USD. When the AUD weakens — as it frequently does — every subscription becomes measurably more expensive, with no corresponding improvement in the service.

On top of that, many US vendors apply an "Australian tax" — pricing AUD subscriptions above the USD equivalent even before exchange rates are applied. It's a double hit that most SMBs haven't quantified.

We benchmark your stack against both AUD and USD market rates, identify local Australian alternatives where they exist, and help you renegotiate terms that reflect the actual value you're receiving.
