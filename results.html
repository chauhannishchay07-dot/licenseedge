15-Person Digital Marketing Agency

The Situation

The agency had grown from 3 to 15 people over 3 years, adding tools reactively as teams requested them. By 2025 they were paying for 23 separate software subscriptions — many with seat counts for employees who had left months earlier.

The founder estimated their software spend at around $40,000/year. The actual figure was $52,400. Over 60% was either unused or duplicated by another tool they were already paying for.

"We knew we were probably overpaying, but we had no idea by how much. LicenseEdge found savings we didn't even know to look for. The ROI on their fee was obvious within the first month."

— Founder, Digital Marketing Agency
